# Software Engineering
## Scope
Software engineering applies disciplined design, testing, collaboration, and operations to maintainable software systems.
## Foundations
Programming, algorithms, data structures, databases, networks, testing, version control, and communication.
## Paths
Backend, frontend, mobile, platform, quality engineering, and AI product engineering share foundations but differ in interfaces and constraints.
