# Embedded Systems
## Scope
Embedded systems combine electronics and software inside products with constraints such as timing, memory, energy, safety, or cost.
## Who may enjoy it
A student who says “I like programming but want to work close to hardware” may enjoy firmware, IoT devices, robotics integration, or edge AI.
## Edge AI
Edge AI runs suitable inference near the sensor. It combines ML evaluation with embedded constraints, model compression, efficient runtimes, and careful measurement.
## Embedded systems versus IoT
An embedded product need not be connected. IoT emphasizes networked devices, device management, data transport, cloud integration, and security across the system.
