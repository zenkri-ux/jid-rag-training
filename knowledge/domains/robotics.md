# Robotics
## Scope
Robotics combines mechanics, electronics, sensing, computation, planning, and control. Different roles emphasize different layers.
## Transition from electrical engineering
Electrical engineering offers circuits, signals, motors, and control foundations. Add Python/C++, Linux, linear algebra, ROS 2, and an integrated robot or simulation project.
## Learning warning
Do not try to master every layer at once. Choose one anchor—control, embedded integration, perception, or planning—while learning enough interfaces to collaborate.
