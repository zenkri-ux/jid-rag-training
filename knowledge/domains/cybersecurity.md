# Cybersecurity
## Scope
Cybersecurity manages risk across people, software, devices, networks, identities, and operations.
## Learning priorities
Begin with networks, Linux, scripting, web behavior, identity, and ethical lab practice. Specialize later in application, cloud, detection, governance, or offensive testing.
## Boundary
Tools do not replace authorization, threat modeling, or careful interpretation of evidence.
