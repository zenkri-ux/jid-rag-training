# Career Transitions
## Preserve transferable foundations
Map existing strengths before collecting new tools: electrical engineers bring signals and control to robotics; backend engineers bring APIs and reliability to AI engineering; analysts bring SQL and domain reasoning to data science.
## Build a bridge project
A bridge project should use an existing strength and demonstrate one target capability. Document design choices, tests, limitations, and what you would improve.
## Avoid title matching
Job titles vary. Compare responsibilities, constraints, and evidence of skill rather than titles alone.
