# Choosing an Engineering Speciality
## Use preferences as hypotheses
Ask what activities create energy: mathematical modeling, product construction, physical systems, data investigation, infrastructure, or adversarial reasoning.
## Test, do not declare
Short projects and conversations with practitioners produce evidence. A recommendation narrows experiments; it should not make an irreversible decision for a student.
## Clarifying dimensions
Mathematics preference, coding preference, hardware interest, research versus products, data interest, infrastructure interest, and desired type of daily work.
