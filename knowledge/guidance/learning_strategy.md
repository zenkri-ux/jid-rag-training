# Learning Strategy
## A repeatable loop
Choose a bounded outcome, learn the minimum concepts needed, build, test, explain failures, then revise.
## Prioritize foundations
Languages and frameworks change. Programming, debugging, data modeling, operating systems, networks, mathematics, testing, and communication transfer across roles.
## Portfolio evidence
Show the problem, constraints, architecture, trade-offs, validation, and limitations—not only a screenshot.
