# Market Overview
## Responsible interpretation
Technology demand differs by location, sector, and time. This workshop intentionally provides no fabricated vacancy, salary, or growth statistics.
## Durable tendency
Employers commonly value evidence that a beginner can learn, build, debug, communicate trade-offs, and work with others. Specific stacks should be checked against current local opportunities.
