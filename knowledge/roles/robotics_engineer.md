# Robotics Engineer
## What the role does
Integrates sensing, planning, control, computation, and mechanics into physical machines.
## Typical responsibilities
Develop perception or control modules, integrate sensors and actuators, simulate, and test safely.
## Core technical skills
Python or C++, linear algebra, control, robotics fundamentals, and systematic integration testing.
## Common technologies
ROS 2, Gazebo, OpenCV, Linux, microcontrollers, and CAD tools depending on the role.
## Useful academic foundations
Control theory, mechanics, electronics, probability, and algorithms.
## Typical beginner projects
A simulated mobile robot that follows waypoints and reports failure states.
## Related roles
Embedded Engineer, Computer Vision Engineer, Automation Engineer.
## Possible transitions
Electrical engineering provides a strong base; add programming, control, and robotics middleware.
## What type of student may enjoy this role
Students who enjoy interdisciplinary, physical, and iterative engineering.
