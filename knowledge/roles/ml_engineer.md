# Machine Learning Engineer
## What the role does
Develops, deploys, and maintains machine-learning systems across training and inference.
## Typical responsibilities
Prepare features, train models, design evaluation, optimize inference, and monitor data or model drift.
## Core technical skills
Python, ML algorithms, statistics, data pipelines, testing, and production software practices.
## Common technologies
PyTorch, scikit-learn, MLflow, Docker, SQL, and cloud compute.
## Useful academic foundations
Probability, linear algebra, optimization, algorithms.
## Typical beginner projects
Train and serve a predictive model, then monitor its errors on changing data.
## Related roles
AI Engineer, Data Scientist, MLOps Engineer.
## Possible transitions
Data scientists often add deployment and software reliability; backend engineers add ML theory.
## What type of student may enjoy this role
Students who like quantitative experiments and engineering systems around models.
