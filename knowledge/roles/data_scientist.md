# Data Scientist
## What the role does
Uses data, statistics, and models to answer questions and support decisions.
## Typical responsibilities
Explore data, form hypotheses, design experiments, model outcomes, and communicate uncertainty.
## Core technical skills
Statistics, Python or R, SQL, visualization, experiment design, and communication.
## Common technologies
pandas, scikit-learn, Jupyter, SQL, and visualization libraries.
## Useful academic foundations
Probability, statistics, linear algebra, domain knowledge.
## Typical beginner projects
Analyze an open dataset and present a reproducible decision-oriented report.
## Related roles
ML Engineer, Data Analyst, Analytics Engineer.
## Possible transitions
Can move toward ML engineering by deepening software deployment skills.
## What type of student may enjoy this role
Students who enjoy ambiguous questions, evidence, and explaining quantitative findings.
